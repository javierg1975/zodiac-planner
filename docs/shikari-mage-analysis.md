# Shikari + Mage Job Analysis

## Two Different Shikari Builds

### 1. Endurance: Penelo (White Mage + Shikari) - **Dark DPS**
**Goal:** Berserked Dark damage for Yiazmat
**Gear:** Yagyu Darkblade + Black Robes + Genji Gloves

### 2. Leader Trinity: Vaan (Red Battlemage + Shikari) - **Evasion Tank**
**Goal:** 90%+ evasion tank with party healing
**Gear:** Main Gauche + Crystal Shield + shields

These are **completely different roles** - let's analyze each separately.

---

## Scenario 1: Penelo's Dark DPS Build

### Requirements
- **Black Robes** (mystic armor) for +50% Dark damage boost
- High Battle Lores for physical DPS
- Genji Gloves synergy for ninja sword combos
- Defensive support (Protectga/Shellga) when not Berserked

### Lore Comparison

| Mage Job | Battle Lores | Magick Lores | Combined | Overlap % |
|----------|--------------|--------------|----------|-----------|
| **White Mage** | 7 (6 shared) | 15 (5 shared) | **22** | High BL overlap |
| **Black Mage** | 6 (0 shared) | 16 (5 shared) | **22** | Zero BL overlap! |
| **Red Battlemage** | 6 (3 shared) | 12 (5 shared) | **18** | Medium overlap |
| **Time Battlemage** | 11 (6 shared) | 9 (5 shared) | **20** | High BL overlap |

### Analysis

**Black Mage is BETTER for lore efficiency:**
- Shikari adds ALL 6 of its Battle Lores (Black Mage has 0)
- White Mage's 7 Battle Lores overlap with Shikari's 6 → Shikari adds 0-1 unique
- Black Mage has 16 Magick Lores (max) vs White Mage's 15

**But White Mage provides:**
- ✅ Healing spells (Cura/Curaga/Curaja) for utility when not Berserked
- ✅ Support spells (Protect/Shell)
- ✅ Cuchulainn unlocks Shikari Protectga/Shellga
- ✅ Better esper compatibility

**Black Mage provides:**
- ✅ Better lore efficiency (Shikari adds 6 Battle Lores instead of 0)
- ✅ Max Magick Lores (16 vs 15)
- ✅ Offensive black magic for non-Berserked phases
- ❌ No healing (must rely on items/other characters)
- ❌ Less defensive utility

### Verdict for Dark DPS Build

**White Mage + Shikari is the correct choice** IF:
- Penelo is your ONLY source of Protectga/Shellga (via Cuchulainn)
- You value healing utility over lore optimization
- The build needs to function when not Berserked

**Black Mage + Shikari is better** IF:
- Another character handles healing/support
- You prioritize lore efficiency (6 extra Battle Lores matters)
- Pure DPS optimization is the goal

For **Endurance specifically**, White Mage is justified because:
1. Ashe (Black Mage + White Mage) is the primary healer
2. Penelo needs Cuchulainn for Protectga/Shellga when NOT Berserked
3. The 6 Battle Lore difference is minor compared to utility

**Rating: White Mage + Shikari = A tier for this role**

## Scenario 2: Vaan's Evasion Tank Build

### Requirements
- **Shields** (for evasion stacking with Main Gauche)
- **Healing spells** (party leader needs to heal)
- High evasion + good armor access
- Battle Lores for when he does attack

### Lore Comparison (Same as above)

### Shield Access

| Mage Job | Shield Access | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| **Red Battlemage** | ✅ Yes | Natural shield access |
| **White Mage** | ✅ Yes | Natural shield access |
| **Time Battlemage** | ❌ No | Mystic armor only |
| **Black Mage** | ❌ No | Mystic armor only |

### Analysis

**Red Battlemage provides:**
- ✅ Shield access (CRITICAL for evasion stacking)
- ✅ Healing magic (Cure/Cura/Curaga)
- ✅ Offensive magic (Fire/Thunder/Blizzaga)
- ✅ Heavy Armor via Exodus (durability)
- ✅ 6 combined Battle Lores (3 RBM + 3 unique from Shikari)

**White Mage provides:**
- ✅ Shield access
- ✅ Better healing magic (Curaja, Esuna, Raise)
- ✅ More support utility (Protect/Shell/Haste)
- ❌ Zero Battle Lores added (Shikari's 6 are subset of WM's 7)
- ✅ 15 Magick Lores (vs RBM's 12)

**Black Mage/Time Battlemage:**
- ❌ No shields → Cannot evasion stack → **Disqualified**

### Verdict for Evasion Tank Build

**Red Battlemage + Shikari is optimal** because:
1. Shield access is **mandatory** for evasion stacking
2. Healing magic is sufficient (Curaga is fine for tank role)
3. Offensive magic provides versatility
4. Heavy Armor (via Exodus) adds durability
5. Better lore efficiency (Shikari adds 3 Battle Lores vs 0 with WM)

**White Mage + Shikari is viable** but:
- Superior healing (Curaja) is overkill for an evasion tank who rarely gets hit
- Loses 3 Battle Lores that RBM combo would get
- Less offensive capability

**Rating: Red Battlemage + Shikari = S tier for evasion tank**

---

## Summary

### Question 1: Is White Mage the best pairing for Shikari in Endurance?

**Answer: YES**, for Penelo's Dark DPS role:
- Black Robes requirement is met ✅
- Cuchulainn unlocks critical Protectga/Shellga ✅
- Healing utility when not Berserked ✅
- 6 Battle Lore efficiency loss is acceptable trade-off ✅

**Alternative:** Black Mage + Shikari would be more lore-efficient BUT loses healing/support utility that Endurance needs.

### Question 2: Is there a better job to complement Vaan's Shikari evasion tank?

**Answer: Red Battlemage is already optimal** for the evasion tank role:
- Shield access is mandatory (eliminates Black Mage/Time Battlemage)
- Red Battlemage has better offense than White Mage
- Red Battlemage adds more unique Battle Lores (3 vs 0)
- Healing magic is sufficient for evasion tank role

**Alternative:** White Mage + Shikari would work but is less optimal (better healing but worse offense/lores).

---

## Key Insight

**The best Shikari pairing depends on the role:**

1. **DPS with Black Robes** → White Mage + Shikari (utility > lore efficiency)
2. **Evasion Tank** → Red Battlemage + Shikari (shields + offense + lores)
3. **Pure lore optimization** → Black Mage + Shikari (zero Battle Lore overlap)
4. **HP Tank** → Would need Knight/Uhlan/Monk for Heavy Armor + HP augments (different strategy entirely)

Both current preset choices are **correct for their intended roles**.

### HP Tank Strategy (Alternative)

If you wanted to build Vaan as an **HP tank instead of evasion tank**, consider:

**Shikari + Knight:**
- Heavy Armor (HP + DEF)
- Shield access
- 12 Battle Lores (6 from Shikari + 6 unique from Knight)
- Healing via Mateus esper (Knight unlocks Curaga/Esuna/Cleanse/Regen)
- **Loses:** Black Robes access (can't boost ninja sword MAG scaling)

**Shikari + Monk:**
- Highest HP augments in game
- ALL 16 Battle Lores (maximum physical stats)
- Healing via Zodiark esper (Monk unlocks Renew)
- **Loses:** Shields (Monk can't equip), Black Robes

**Verdict:** For HP tank, **Shikari + Knight** is best (shields + heavy armor + healing). But this changes the role from "evasion tank" to "damage sponge" - different strategy entirely.
