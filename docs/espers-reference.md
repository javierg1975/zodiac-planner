# Esper Allocation Reference Guide

Quick reference guide for optimal esper assignments in Final Fantasy XII: The Zodiac Age.

## General Allocation Rules

**If the character already has benefits of the Esper, move onto the next listed thing. If there is no next thing, then that Esper can be allocated as you please, as all other bonuses would be insignificant.**

## Strategic Esper Assignments by Build Type

### Healer-Focused Builds
- **Knight healer**: Mateus (Curaga), Hashmal (Curaja/buffs)
- **Monk healer**: Chaos (Holy/support), Zodiark (Renew), Ultima (Swiftness)
- **White Mage primary**: Keep core spells, assign Chaos to Monk if possible
- **Time Battlemage healer**: Adrammelech (Cura/Raise), Famfrit (Hastega)

### DPS Optimization
- **Monk DPS**: Ultima (Swiftness 3) is CRITICAL for maximum attack speed
- **Red Battlemage DPS**: Cuchulainn (tier 3 elemental magic), Zeromus (Channeling 3)
- **Black Mage DPS**: Exodus (Channeling 3), Chaos (Renew for sustain)
- **Physical DPS with Swiftness**: Check if job naturally has Swiftness 3 or needs Ultima

### Tank/Survivability
- **Shikari tank**: Cuchulainn (Remedy Lore 3), Famfrit (Heavy Armor)
- **Knight tank**: Mateus (healing), Hashmal (buffs)
- **HP boost priorities**: Zalera (+435 HP), Exodus (+500 HP), Zeromus (+230 HP)

### Support/Utility
- **Hastega access**: Famfrit (Machinist/Time Battlemage), Chaos (White Mage), Zodiark (Knight)
- **Break abilities**: Cuchulainn (Time Battlemage), Zeromus (Monk)
- **Channeling 3 (infinite MP)**: Exodus (White/Black Mage), Zeromus (Monk/Machinist)

## Key Augments and Abilities

### Swiftness
- Increases character speed by ~10% per rank (max 3 ranks)
- **Swiftness 3 jobs**: Archer, Bushi, Shikari, Machinist
- **Swiftness 1-2 jobs**: Uhlan, Knight, Foebreaker, Monk (needs Ultima for rank 3)
- **Critical for**: Berserked physical DPS optimization
- **Effect with Berserk**: ~5% DPS increase per rank (vs. 10% without Berserk)

### Channeling 3
- 10% chance to cast spells for 0 MP (effectively infinite MP)
- **Best for**: Black Mage (high spell usage), Red Battlemage (MP issues)
- **Unlocked by**: Exodus (White/Black Mage), Zeromus (Monk/Machinist)

### Hastega
- Party-wide Haste buff (1.2x speed without Berserk, 1.1x with Berserk)
- **Priority**: Time Battlemage (natural), then Machinist (Famfrit)
- **Alternative**: White Mage (Chaos), Knight (Zodiark)
- **Note**: High MP cost, obtained very late game

### Heavy Armor Access
- Increases physical damage by ~1.5x (STR scaling)
- **Critical unlocks**:
  - Archer: Shemhazai (Heavy Armor 10-12) + Firaga
  - Red Battlemage: Exodus (Heavy Armor 8-10) if paired with light armor job
  - Shikari: Famfrit (Heavy Armor 3-8) for tank builds

## Common Build Patterns

### The "Monk Trinity" (Optimal Monk Build)
1. **Ultima** → Swiftness 3 (MANDATORY for DPS)
2. **Chaos** → Holy, Protectga, Shellga, Esunaga
3. **Zodiark** → Renew (ultimate heal)

This gives Monk maximum physical DPS with full support capabilities.

### Knight as Secondary Healer
1. **Mateus** → Curaga, Regen, Cleanse, Esuna (early healing)
2. **Hashmal** → Curaja, Faith, Bravery, Confuse (upgraded healing + buffs)

Transforms Knight into competent healer with full buff suite.

### Red Battlemage as Primary Mage
1. **Cuchulainn** → Firaga, Thundaga, Blizzaga, Sleepga (tier 3 magic)
2. **Zeromus** → Channeling 3 (infinite MP for sustained casting)
3. **Exodus** → Heavy Armor 8-10 (if paired with Shikari/light class)

Creates powerful magic DPS with sustainability and survivability.

### Time Battlemage as Support
1. **Adrammelech** → Cura, Raise (early healing capability)
2. **Famfrit** → Hastega (party speed boost)

Time Battlemage naturally has excellent support spells; these fill healing gaps.

## Allocation Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't give Ultima to non-Monk jobs** - Swiftness 3 has minimal value unless job lacks it naturally
2. **Don't skip Cuchulainn on Red Battlemage** - Tier 3 elemental magic is their primary DPS
3. **Don't waste Chaos on White Mage** - Monk benefits more from the support spells
4. **Don't give Channeling 3 to jobs with low spell usage** - Best on Black/Red Mage
5. **Don't forget Mateus + Hashmal on Knight healers** - Both are essential for healing progression

## Esper Acquisition Timeline

Espers become available at different points in the game. Plan your license board progression accordingly.

**Early Game**:
- Belias (automatic story)
- Mateus (Stilshrine of Miriam)

**Mid Game**:
- Adrammelech (Zertinan Caverns)
- Zalera (Barheim Passage)
- Shemhazai (Giruvegan)

**Late Game**:
- Hashmal (Pharos)
- Cuchulainn (Garamsythe Waterway)
- Zeromus (Stillshrine of Miriam - Special)
- Exodus (Mosphoran Highwaste)

**Endgame**:
- Famfrit (Pharos)
- Chaos (Necrohol of Nabudis)
- Ultima (Giruvegan)
- Zodiark (Henne Mines - Optional superboss)

## Notes

- Each esper can only be assigned to ONE character
- Esper assignments are permanent (cannot be changed without resetting licenses)
- Some builds require specific espers to function optimally
- Plan your entire party's esper allocation before committing
- Priority espers (bold in table) should be assigned first to their optimal jobs
- Remaining espers can be allocated more flexibly based on your party composition
