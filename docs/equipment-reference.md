## Armor Types & Bonuses

In Final Fantasy XII, there are three armor types with distinct stat bonuses:

| **Light Armor** | **Heavy Armor** | **Mystic Armor** |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Increases HP | Increases STR | Increases MAG and MP |
| Balanced DEF and MDEF | Higher DEF than MDEF | Higher MDEF than DEF |

### Key Points:

- **Heavy Armor** and **Mystic Armor** are critical for maximizing effectiveness
- Level-appropriate armor adds **~25% per piece** to physical or magical effectiveness
- Combined effect: **~50% total damage/healing increase** when wearing stat-appropriate gear
- This bonus remains significant until stats are maxed at level 99

### Notable Armor Pieces

**Heavy Armor:**
- **Grand Armor** - Heavy Armor 12 unlock (high STR bonus, best non-Genji armor)
- **Genji Armor** - High defense, works well for evasion builds
- **Maximillian** - +9 STR/+6 SPD (Trial Mode Stage 45, useful for stat maxing)

**Mystic Armor:**
- **Black Robes** - Boosts Dark element damage by 50% (Necrohol of Nabudis, ~35% game)
- **White Robes** - Boosts Holy element damage by 50% (Pharos, ~90% game)
- **Magepower Shishak** - High MAG bonus (Trial Mode Stage 43)
- **Lordly Robes** - High MAG/MP for casters

***Knight is more of an endgame class, but Karkata provides good early-mid game damage.

**Note:** Late game physical classes exceed early game classes in damage, but early game classes still have sufficient DPS for late game content.

### Fast vs. Slow Physical Classes

Use this pairing table for optimal Physical DPS builds:

| **"Slow" Classes** | **"Fast" Classes** |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Uhlan | Archer |
| Knight | Shikari |
| Monk | Bushi |
| Foebreaker* | Machinist |
| | Time Battlemage |

- "Fast" classes have Swiftness 3, low base STR, early weapon access
- "Slow" classes have Swiftness 1-2, high base STR (via Heavy Armor), late weapon access

## Critical Accessories

### Genji Gloves

**Effect:** Boosts combo rate by 1.8x for multi-hit weapons

**Critical for:**
- Katana users (Bushi with Masamune/Kumbha)
- Pole users (Monk with Kanya/Dragon Whisker)
- Ninja sword users (Shikari with daggers)

**DPS Impact:**
- 1.25x damage on Kanya/Katanas
- 1.05-1.10x on other combo weapons

**NOT useful with:** Bows, crossbows, guns, hand-bombs, rods, or measures (these weapons cannot combo)

**Acquisition:** Rare drop/steal from Gilgamesh fight

### Germinas Boots

**Effect:** Increases Speed-based weapon damage significantly

**DPS Impact:** ~1.5x damage multiplier for SPD weapons

**Best for:**
- Archer (bows scale with SPD)
- Shikari with certain ninja swords

**Acquisition:** Henne Mines/Trial Mode

### Bubble Belt

**Effect:** Max HP boost

**Critical for:** Superboss survival (Hell Wyrm, Yiazmat, Omega Mark XII)

**Use case:** Long fights where survival is more important than damage optimization

### Ribbon

**Effect:** Complete status immunity

**Critical for:** Long endgame fights where status effects are prevalent

**Priority:** Essential for healers and leaders in difficult content

### Berserk Bracers

**Effect:** Permanent Berserk status

**DPS Impact:** ~2.0x damage multiplier (1.5x damage, 2x speed)

**Trade-off:** Character becomes uncontrollable and only auto-attacks

**Best for:** Pure physical DPS characters (no magic/healing needed)

**Acquisition:** Trial Mode Stage 5 (obtainable after getting all 6 party members)

### Cameo Belt

**Effect:** Greatly increases accuracy against evasive enemies

**DPS Impact:** 1.25-1.40x effective damage against enemies with evasion

**Use case:** Critical for endgame bosses with high evasion

**Notable Enemies:**
- Trial Mode Stage 100 (30-40 Evasion)
- Lv. 99 Red Chocobos (~40 Evasion)
- Abysteels (30-40 Evasion)
- Omega Mark XII
- Zodiark (<50% HP)
- Gilgamesh/Enkidu

### Hermes Sandals

**Effect:** +5 STR, auto-Haste

**Use case:** General purpose accessory for physical DPS when Genji Gloves isn't needed

### Sage's Ring

**Effect:** MP boost and regeneration

**Best for:** Magic-heavy characters (Black Mage, White Mage, Red Battlemage)

## Elemental Equipment & Boosting

**Key Mechanic:** Elemental boosting equipment increases damage by **50%** with that element, even if the enemy isn't weak to it.

### Elemental Boosting Equipment Table

| **Time (% Game)** | **Class** | **Equipment** | **Element** | **Acquisition** |
|-------------------|-----------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| The Leviathan (~15%) | Black Mage | Cherry Staff | Wind | Shop |
| After The Leviathan (~15%) | Archer | Burning Bow | Fire | Hunt |
| Jahara (~35%) | Black Mage | Flame Staff | Fire | Shop |
| Eruyt Village (~40%) | Black Mage | Storm Staff | Lightning | Shop |
| Mt Bur-Omisace (~45%) | Black Mage | Glacial Staff | Ice | Shop |
| After Raithwall (~35%, but hard) | White Mage, Black Mage, Red Battlemage, Bushi | Black Robes | Dark | Necrohol of Nabudis (Treasure) |
| Pharos (~90%) | White Mage, Black Mage, Red Battlemage, Bushi | White Robes | Holy | Pharos (Treasure) |

### Key Observations:

- **Black Mage** has access to almost all elemental boosting equipment, contributing to their high magic damage
- **Red Battlemage's** lack of elemental staff access is why they deal less magic damage
- Elemental weapons with boosting armor are competitive with non-elemental weapons in most scenarios

### Elemental Weapon Combinations

**Knight + Mystic Armor Class:**
- **Excalibur** (Holy damage) + **White Robes** = 50% damage boost
- Pairing: Knight/White Mage, Knight/Black Mage, Knight/Red Battlemage, Knight/Bushi

**Shikari + Mystic Armor Class:**
- **Yagyu Darkblade** (Dark damage) + **Black Robes** = 50% damage boost
- Pairing: Shikari/Black Mage, Shikari/Red Battlemage, Shikari/Bushi

**Machinist + Mystic Armor Class:**
- **Dark Shot** (Dark damage) + **Black Robes** = 50% damage boost
- Pairing: Machinist/Black Mage, Machinist/Red Battlemage

**Uhlan (Optional):**
- **Holy Lance** + **White Robes** = 50% damage boost
- Note: Holy Lance is weaker than Zodiac Spear, but useful for Holy-weak enemies

**Endgame Note:** Elemental damage is often resisted in endgame, and Dark is especially resisted. However, these combinations are still very useful for specific fights.

## Buff & Item DPS Multipliers

Understanding these multipliers is critical for maximizing damage:

| **Buff/Item** | **Value** | **Condition/Notes** |
|--------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Adrenaline** | 2.0x | <25% HP (extremely powerful) |
| **5% HP Combo Rate** | ~1.5x | ~5% HP (combo-based weapons) |
| **Berserk** | ~2.0x | Uncontrollable, physical attacks only |
| **Heavy Armor** | ~1.5x | Pure STR weapon, <99 STR |
| **Germinas Boots** | ~1.5x | SPD-based weapons |
| **Bravery** | 1.3x | STR buff spell |
| **Cameo Belt** | 1.25-1.40x | Enemies with evasion |
| **Genji Gloves** | 1.25x on Kanya/Katanas | 1.05-1.10x on other combo weapons |
| **Haste** | 1.1x (with Berserk) | 1.2x without Berserk |
| **Swiftness** | 1.05x per rank (Berserk) | 1.1x per rank (no Berserk) |
| **Focus** | Variable | Increases combo rate at full HP |

**Key Combinations:**
- **Berserk + Bravery + Haste** = ~2.5x base DPS
- **Adrenaline + Combo weapons + Genji Gloves** = Extreme burst DPS
- **Heavy Armor + Pure STR weapon** = Consistent high DPS throughout game

## Notes & Tips

### On Genji Gloves
- Only useful with combo weapons (ninja swords, poles, katanas, Wyrmhero Blade)
- Does NOT work with bows, crossbows, guns, hand-bombs, rods, or measures
- Most players will only have 1-2 pairs total

### On Elemental Weapons
- 50% damage boost is powerful but situational
- Endgame content often resists elements (especially Dark)
- Holy and Dark are most frequently useful in endgame
- Keep non-elemental weapon as backup

### On Armor Specialization
- Heavy Armor for Physical DPS (STR boost)
- Mystic Armor for Mages (MAG boost) and elemental weapon users
- Don't mix armor types - specialization is more effective

### On Accessories
- Genji Gloves: Combo weapon users
- Germinas Boots: SPD weapon users (Archer, some Shikari)
- Bubble Belt: Superboss survival
- Ribbon: Status-heavy fights
- Cameo Belt: High evasion enemies
- Berserk Bracers: Pure physical DPS (no control needed)

### Weapon Acquisition Philosophy
- Physical DPS value is heavily weapon-dependent
- Don't bother with weapons you won't acquire
- Early game classes remain viable in late game with proper buffs
- Focus on 2-3 strong weapon progressions rather than all 12 jobs

## Damage Formula Reference

**Physical/Magical Damage:**
```
DMG = (ATK * rand(1, 1.25) - DEF) * (1 + MAIN * (LVL + SUB) / CONST)
```

**Where:**
- ATK = Weapon/spell power
- DEF = Enemy defense
- MAIN = Main stat (STR for most weapons, MAG for spells/katanas)
- LVL = Character level
- SUB = Secondary stat (usually same as MAIN, sometimes SPD/VIT/MAG)
- CONST = 256 (STR/MAG), 218 (SPD), 128 (VIT)

**Key Insight:** Character stat bonuses from armor multiply the entire second part of the formula, making them extremely valuable until level 99.
