# Advanced Tactics Guide

This guide covers intermediate to advanced strategies for Final Fantasy XII: The Zodiac Age. These tactics are primarily focused on doing difficult areas early for their incredible rewards and maximizing party effectiveness through specialized mechanics.

**Target Audience**: Players with experience in FFXII who want to tackle difficult content early or optimize their party composition for specific challenges.

## Doing Difficult Areas Early

Final Fantasy XII allows you to sequence break optional areas to a certain extent. This is the primary reason why you can obtain strong weapons and magic early in the game.

**Key Benefits:**
- Rewards for doing difficult areas early (even without completing bosses) are often significantly better than hunts available at the same time
- Early access to powerful equipment and spells provides massive advantages
- Early game optimization can be more challenging than endgame content due to limited resources

**Important**: If you do not want to do difficult areas early, the rest of this guide is somewhat unnecessary. However, if you want to tackle difficult content early on, continue reading.

## The Leader Playstyle

This playstyle centers around putting all damage onto 1 character (the "Tank" or "Leader") to maximize the effectiveness of HP% augments on the other two party members.

### Core Concept

Because HP% augments provide massive damage and healing bonuses, you should try to distribute as much damage as possible to 1 character in the party, rather than having all characters take damage.

**Why This Works:**
- Other party members can maintain full HP and benefit from Focus/Serenity (1.5x damage/healing multipliers)
- You effectively gain another character's worth of effectiveness by concentrating damage
- There's no real opportunity cost for building a team capable of this strategy

### Leader Requirements

The Leader role has these requirements:
1. Take the team's damage
2. Don't die
3. Support the team as much as possible without expecting to maintain HP% augments

**Best Practice**: Lead the party with your Leader character, as enemies often attack the first character they see. Avoid using Berserked Physical DPSs as Leaders since you cannot directly control them.

**Natural Synergy**: Healers make excellent Leaders since they need to support the team anyway. Have the other two party members double down on damage (magical or physical).

## HP% Condition Strategies

Various augments and mechanics greatly increase a character's effectiveness when they meet specific HP conditions.

### HP% Augment Breakdown

**Focus/Serenity (Full HP):**
- **Focus**: Multiplies physical damage by 1.5x when HP is full
- **Serenity**: Multiplies magical damage and healing by 1.5x when HP is full (also makes magical status effects 2.0x more likely to hit)

**Adrenaline/Spellbreaker (HP < 25%):**
- **Adrenaline**: Multiplies physical damage by 2.0x when HP is less than 25%
- **Spellbreaker**: Multiplies magical damage by 2.0x and healing by 1.5x when HP is less than 25% (also makes magical status effects 2.0x more likely to hit)

**Physical Weapon Combo Rate:**
- Most physical weapons have a chance to hit multiple times (combo)
- When you combo, the number of hits depends on your HP (more hits at lower HP)
- **Combos are longest when HP is less than 6.25%**
- Combined with Adrenaline, Physical DPS can deal massive damage

### Multiplicative Stacking

These bonuses multiplicatively stack with other bonuses like Bravery/Faith and Berserk. **Focus/Serenity in particular is almost always worth maintaining** as it's fairly safe and easy to maintain in most situations.

### HP% Augments by Class

| **Focus/Adrenaline** | **Serenity/Spellbreaker** | **None** |
|----------------------|---------------------------|----------|
| Uhlan | White Mage | Machinist |
| Knight | Black Mage | |
| Monk | Red Battlemage | |
| Foebreaker | Time Battlemage | |
| Shikari | Bushi | |
| Archer* | | |

*Archer is missing Adrenaline for some reason.

**Important Notes:**
- Most job combinations will get you the relevant augments
- Avoid pairing Archer, Bushi, and Machinist together for physical DPS (they're all "Fast" classes and lack some physical HP augments)
- Pair Knight and Monk with Mage classes if you want to heal with them (Serenity/Spellbreaker boost healing significantly)
- Dark Robe Machinist combos miss Focus and Adrenaline, but you can equip these as accessories

## Evasion Builds

### Common Misconception

It's a misconception that evasion is worthless because hunts and bosses ignore it. While most hunts and bosses ignore physical evasion, **normal enemies almost never ignore evasion**.

### Why Evasion Matters

**For Early Endgame Areas:**
- Going to endgame areas early with high evasion greatly decreases damage taken
- Allows you to complete areas and collect treasure much more easily
- Some endgame normal enemies have stats equal to or exceeding hunts available at the same time

**Survivability Increases DPS:**
- Characters taking less damage need less healing
- More time spent dealing damage instead of healing
- If distributing all damage to 1 character, evasion helps them survive

### Evasion in the Endgame

Evasion becomes less important in true endgame content because:
- No more optional areas to do early
- Access to abundant resources (gear, levels, consumables)
- Can brute force content with enough time investment

**Exception**: Abysteel junction in Henne Mines Part 2 (the best spot for level grinding) still benefits from evasion builds.

## Best Leader Combinations

### Red Battlemage/Shikari (Early to Mid Game)

**Why This Works:**
- Shikari has access to **Main Gauche** (50% evasion dagger available at ~35% game progress in Jahara)
- Can still wear a shield with Main Gauche
- Red Battlemage is the only single class that can heal AND deal magical damage
- Ignores Main Gauche's poor attack rating by using magic

**vs. Shikari/White Mage:**
- White Mage/Shikari has MP issues while using Main Gauche
- Main Gauche does low damage, causing sustainability problems
- Red Battlemage deals more DPS in early/mid game with spells while wearing Mystic Armor
- Shikari/White Mage does less endgame Physical DPS due to lack of Heavy Armor

**Fire Boosted Ardor Note:**
- Firaga has similar DPS to Ardor (Ardor has 50% more power but 80% longer animation time)
- Burning Bow/Dhanusha outdamages Ardor when Berserked for Fire damage
- Makes pairing Red Battlemage with Archer or Black Mage awkward (no real DPS increase)
- Physical DPS outdamages magic in endgame
- Bosses immune to Physical (Chaos, Ultima, Zodiark) are also immune to Fire or use Shift

### Knight/Black Mage (Endgame Only)

**The Endgame Evolution:**
- Endgame version of Shikari/Red Battlemage
- Uses shields with 50+ evasion instead of Main Gauche
- Not as tanky theoretically, but still very tanky

**Superior Endgame Capabilities:**
- Access to endgame White Magic (Bravery, Faith)
- Can take over as evasion tank while maintaining healing and buffs
- Non-elemental spells (can't use elemental staves with shields, but has best spell options)
- Serves as one of the best Magical DPSs in the game
- Also one of the best Physical DPSs (Knight + Mystic Armor for Holy Boosted Excalibur)

**vs. Knight/Bushi:**
- Bushi has 3 Swiftness Lores vs. Black Mage's 2
- Only 5% Physical DPS difference while berserked
- Knight/Black Mage is much more versatile when non-berserked
- Much better before getting Tier 2+ weapons

### Monk/Time Battlemage (Mid to Endgame)

**Different Approach:**
- Massive HP pool
- Less evasion and worse healing than the other two combinations
- Still plenty durable for normal gameplay

**Healing Trade-offs:**
- Lacks Mystic Armor and Faith (healing not as effective for most of the game)
- More "theoretical" limit than practical (Curaja restores a lot of HP)
- Eventually gets **Renew** (restores 100% HP regardless of MAG or Faith)

**Damage Output:**
- Won't reach Red Battlemage/Shikari early game heights
- Won't reach Knight/Black Mage late game heights
- Still incredibly solid as a damage dealer throughout

**Ultimate Team Support:**
- **Best Physical DPS utility in the game**
- Only class combination with: Haste, Bravery, Expose, Renew, and Reverse
- Maximizes Physical DPS **as a team** better than any other combination

**Genji Gloves Question:**
- Haste contributes ~20% DPS for non-berserked Physical DPSs
- Genji Gloves provides 25% DPS on Kanya only
- Hasted Monk/Time Battlemage has similar individual DPS to non-hasted Genji Gloves Monk
- Providing Haste to entire team makes up the 5% difference (higher overall DPS)
- Before 99 STR with Genji Armor, Monk/Time Battlemage deals far more damage (better Heavy Armor access)
- Much better support throughout the entire game
- As Berserked Genji Gloves DPS, Bushi and Knight are better options than Monk

### Leader Gambit Setup

1. Ally: HP < X%: Strongest Healing Spell (<40% for secondary Healer, <70% for main Healer)
2. Ally: Any: Esuna
3. Ally: Any: Raise/Arise
4. **Self: Decoy** (main difference from Spellcaster gambits)
5. DPS: Appropriate damage buffs (Bravery, Faith, Berserk, Haste/ga, etc)
6. Ally: Any: Defensive buffs (Protect/ga, Shell/ga)
7. Any Enemy: HP = 100%: Steal
8. Spells that exploit enemy weaknesses (optional)
9. Attack or Strongest Magic Spell
10. Self: Quality of Life buffs (Libra, Float)
11. Any Ally: Cura (tops off party members to trigger Focus/Serenity)

### Main Healer vs. Secondary Healer

**Leader as Main Healer:**
- Leader heals less (due to not maintaining HP% augments)
- Secondary healer can deal more damage
- Best for easy areas (higher DPS)

**Leader as Secondary Healer:**
- Secondary healer deals less damage (healing instead)
- Team has more overall healing
- Best for difficult areas (better survivability)

## Animation Time Optimization

### When It Matters

Animation time optimization is **mainly only relevant** for combo classes at 5% HP in the endgame:
- Shikari
- Monk
- Bushi
- Foebreaker (relevance questionable in endgame)

### Character Rankings by Class

| **Shikari** | **Monk** | **Bushi** | **Foebreaker** |
|-------------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Basch | Fran | Basch | Ashe |
| Balthier | Basch | Balthier | Basch |
| Penelo | Vaan | Vaan | |

**Notes:**
- Basch and Balthier are good with most weapons and have high Strength (make them your physical DPSs)
- Difference between fast and slow animation characters: ~5-10% DPS
- Not a huge deal in practice

### Should You Optimize?

**For Beginners**: No need to optimize animation times.

**For Advanced Players**: Only if you plan to use 5% HP strategies in endgame, which is a very limited use case.

## 4x Speed Considerations

### The Single Largest DPS Increase

The ability to use 4x speed most of the time is literally the **SINGLE largest DPS increase in the game**.

Note: In *aggregate*, you can increase DPS past 4x, but as a **SINGLE** factor, nothing beats 4x speed.

### When You Can't Use 4x Speed

You lose massive DPS when you have to menu during combat. Reasons include:

**Survivability Issues:**
- On 4x Speed, you take 4x damage
- Team dying forces you to menu (swap characters, redo content)
- If team isn't survivable enough to play on 4x, you've lost DPS

**Low HP Strategies:**
- Getting party members to extremely low HP for high damage is awkward with gambits alone until endgame
- **Not worth it for the vast majority of fights**, especially if characters die

**MP Management:**
- Siphoning MP from characters with excess MP
- Very relevant for healers without MP regeneration
- This is why pairing main healers with damage capabilities is valuable (damage translates to MP with passives)

**Strategic Adjustments:**
- Changing gambits or equipment for specific strategies/weaknesses
- Reflect strategies
- Changing elemental boosting weapons
- Swapping accessories (Genji Gloves vs. Cameo Belt)

**Non-Active Party Management:**
- Using items/spells on non-active party members requires menuing

### Real-Time DPS vs. In-Game DPS

**Critical Concept:**
- Some strategies have **higher in-game DPS** but **lower real-time DPS** due to heavy menuing
- Primary example: Manually reducing HP for high DPS strategies
- Even ignoring death risk, it's usually not worth the **real world time**
- Most fights are short anyway

**Don't Overvalue 5% HP Strategies:**
- Silly to evaluate weapons entirely based on 5% HP performance
- That encompasses so little of actual weapon use
- "lol 4x Speed" with normal use is often faster than optimized low HP strategies

### Caveats

**When 4x Speed Doesn't Help:**
- Fights with time limits (Demon Wall fights)
- In these cases, maximize **in-game DPS** over **real-world DPS**

**When to Maximize Theoretical DPS:**
- Superbosses like Hell Wyrm and Yiazmat
- These are incredibly limited scenarios
- Not the norm for most gameplay

### Practical Takeaway

**Prioritize survivability and minimal menuing** to maintain 4x speed as much as possible. This will give you better real-world clear times than trying to min-max low HP strategies for most content.

## Recommended Team Combinations

You can use all three leader combinations in your party with no real opportunity cost. They're already almost optimal as DPSs anyway:

1. Red Battlemage/Shikari
2. Knight/Black Mage
3. Monk/Time Battlemage

**Final Three Characters** (any will work, but these add versatility):
- Another Healer (White Mage)
- Another Elemental Boosted Magical DPS (Black Mage)
- Physical DPSs to fill remaining slots

**Specific Examples:**
- White Mage/Black Mage (covers first two roles)
- Bushi/Knight (Physical DPS, permanently berserked)
- Archer/Uhlan (Physical DPS, permanently berserked)

**For 12 Jobs:**
- White Mage/Machinist
- Bushi/Foebreaker
- Uhlan/Archer

Any variation of these classes works well.
